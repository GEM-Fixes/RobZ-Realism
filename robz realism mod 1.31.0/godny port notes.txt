==================== MULTIPLAYER BOT REWRITE ====================
Files (resource\gamelogic.pak, script/multiplayer/):
  bot.lua        - new bot brain (replaces the vanilla script)
  bot.data.lua   - settings (BotConfig, BotComposition, ...). Set Debug = false to turn off the chat status and log.
  bot.units.lua  - generated: every buyable squad/vehicle per army with role, cost and years
  bot.maps.lua   - generated: flag and spawn positions for every MP map
Backup of the pack before this change: %TEMP%\godnyport\gamelogic_before_ai.pak

Why the old bot "gave up": 167 of its 614 shopping-list entries do not exist in this mod, and most of the rest are
for other years. The vanilla script retries a failed purchase forever, so the first bad pick froze its buying for
the rest of the game.

What the engine lets a bot do: buy a unit, and send a group to a flag. Nothing else (no positions, no enemy info,
no orders to single soldiers). All tactics are built from those two commands:
  - buying: picks from the real unit list, gives up on units it cannot buy and learns the game's year,
    follows a target army mix that changes over time (infantry early, tanks/TDs later, more AT if enemy has tanks)
  - squads are split into groups of 4 so parts can hold and attack different flags
  - opening: spreads out and grabs the nearest neutral flags
  - holds flags, more groups on front-line flags, best holders (MGs, AT guns) stay, tanks go on the attack
  - attacks one main flag at a time: gathers at the nearest held flag, launches when strong enough,
    calls it off if it stalls, avoids flags where it keeps losing groups, counter-attacks flags it just lost
  - extra strength goes to a second target
  - map knowledge: it reads the map name from game.log and uses flag positions; if it can't, it uses map-free rules
Debug log: Documents\my games\men of war - assault squad 2\log\robz_bot_<player>.log

Bot update: infantry now split to single soldiers (InfantrySplit = 1), vehicles keep their crew (VehicleSplit = 6). Holding/attack sizes count strength (1 = 4 soldiers) instead of groups.

Bot update: HoldFlags = false - the bot no longer sends groups to flags its team holds (no garrisons, no rallying at own flags); all groups go for neutral and enemy flags. Bot log now appends and also prints into game.log. Engine limit found: a bought squad arrives as at most 2 groups; the K 'disband' is a player-only command. Packs are now installed with %TEMP%\godnyport\install_pak.ps1 (clean rebuild).

Bot update: split test showed the engine makes at most 2 groups per purchase (test now off, SplitTest = false). Found that Spawn says 'yes' even for units the game can't find; the bot now only counts a purchase when its soldiers arrive, checks game.log for 'Unit Not found' 2 s after ordering, and skips such units.

Bot update: buys as soon as it can afford something. If the preferred unit can't be bought it immediately buys an affordable alternative (AltTriesPerSecond); it only saves for a vehicle once it has 8+ groups, for at most 60 s (SaveForVehicles, SaveMinGroups, SaveMaxTime).

--- 2026-10-29: bot unit control DLL (robzbot.dll) ---
robzbot.dll (in this mod folder) is loaded by script/multiplayer/bot.lua with package.loadlib. It calls mowas_2.exe 3.262.1
functions at fixed addresses (from the AS2 reconstruction); nothing in the exe is patched. Only the host needs it.
Adds: per-unit Move (with run = double-click flag), Disband squad (same as K), Attack, own unit list, visible enemies,
map point positions. bot.lua: squads are disbanded on arrival, every soldier/vehicle is planned separately, long moves
run, units slow to walking near enemies, attacks stage on the ground short of the target flag, front flags are screened
from in front, AT units/tanks target visible enemy vehicles. Settings in bot.data.lua (UseRobzDll=false turns it off).
Without the DLL the bot plays the old way. Source: robzbot.c next to the DLL (build: i686-w64-mingw32-clang -O2 -shared -static -o robzbot.dll robzbot.c -Wl,--kill-at).

== 2026-10-29: AI v5 - combat reactions, firing lines, single-buy limit (robzbot.dll v5) ==
- Distance fix: the game uses 20 units per metre. The old "enemy is close" checks were ~5 m, so units never reacted and kept sprinting (sprinting soldiers don't shoot). All distances in bot.data.lua are rescaled (comments give metres).
- Engaged soldiers (enemy seen within 70 m, or taking damage) stop, take the best cover within 16 m facing the enemy (sandbags, walls, craters, via the game's own cover search + take-cover order), or else go prone and fight.
- Fire and movement: attackers that outnumber what they can see move up 16 m at a time, preferably cover to cover; half the line moves while the other half fires.
- Attacks form a line across the approach about 15 m outside a defended flag, 3.5 m apart. They assault when they outnumber the defenders 1.3:1 or after 45 s. Holders and garrison men take cover at their spot; holders stay inside the zone.
- Units walk, not run, when any enemy is within 90 m of them or their destination.
- One-man purchases: at most one every 30 s, the same one every 90 s, and no more than 25% of soldiers.
- New DLL functions: Pose, Covers, Cover. Config: Combat=false turns all of this off.

== 2026-10-29: AI v6 - capture teams, dug-in lines, sandbags, machine-gun range (robzbot.dll v6) ==
- The v5 script did not reach the game on the first try: the pak was built from the wrong folder. It was reinstalled and checked.
- Contested flags (middle and enemy back flags): 3 riflemen go into the zone to capture or hold it. Everyone else gets the task "dig": a line 10 m behind the zone, 6 m apart, spread sideways toward the neighbouring flags. While a flag is still defended, the whole attack line fights together first.
- Soldiers who carry sandbags build a 5 m wall just in front of their spot, facing the enemy, using the game's barricade action. They then take cover behind it. They don't start building while an enemy is in sight.
- Cover choice favours places they can fire from: low cover such as sandbags, low walls and craters, a wall that is actually between them and the enemy, and tall walls only near an end where they can lean out.
- Machine gunners (any soldier carrying a weapon tagged mgun): they engage out to 135 m. If an enemy comes within 95 m they fall back to about 115 m. They dig in 65 m behind contested flags and stay 30 m behind attack lines.
- New DLL: Build(), plus mg/bags in Units(). Settings are in bot.data.lua: CaptureTeam, DigBack, BuildSandbags, MGKeep/MGMin/MGWant/MGMax and others.

== 2026-10-29: AI v7 - sandbag lines at 25/50 m, sandbag-first cover, close combat, smoke (robzbot.dll v7) ==
- Sandbag builders keep building under fire, and keep walking to the spot they build at. After 30 s they take cover behind their own bags.
- Dug-in spots sit in two bands, 25 m and 50 m behind the flag point, spread sideways and facing the enemy. A spot whose view of the flag is blocked (ray trace: buildings, walls, trees) is slid sideways in 4 m steps until it is clear.
- Cover: sandbags first (recognised by the entity name), until 3 of our men are already using that sandbag. A cover point on the enemy's side of a wall is never chosen, so men sit behind the bags, not in front.
- Machine gunners are pushed up: they dig in 35 m behind a flag and stay 12 m behind attack lines. They fall back only inside 85 m (to about 105 m), move up when nothing is within 105 m, and are never sent to a spot inside 85 m of a known enemy.
- Close combat: an enemy soldier within 40 m triggers a grenade if the soldier has one (at most every 20 s), then a run at the enemy, then an attack order. Men with anti-tank grenades use them on vehicles within 40 m. Gunners and snipers don't do this.
- Smoke: a middle or enemy back flag with enemies on it and at least 2 of our men fighting for it gets a smoke grenade on the flag point. It is thrown by our nearest soldier within 45 m who has smoke, at most every 40 s per flag.
- New DLL: Throw (grenade, AT grenade, smoke) and Clear (line of sight). Covers now gives the owning entity's id and name. Units gives grenade, AT grenade and smoke counts.
